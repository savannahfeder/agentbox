# Launch plan

We launch on Tuesday morning with one post, one film and one email to the waiting list.

## What we are launching

Teams of people and agents working in the same docs, tables and threads, live. Everyone sees who is in a file and where they are typing.

## Before the day

- Finish the pricing page and have two customers read it.
- Record the film on the real app, with the whole team in one doc.
- Write the post and the email, and have three people read both.
- Check every link in the post opens on a phone.

## On the day

1. Post at 9 in the morning.
2. Email the waiting list at 10.
3. Answer every reply by the evening.
4. Write down every question we could not answer.

## Who does what

The post is Maya's. The film is Ben's. The email is Ana's. Agents draft the replies and a person sends each one.

## What we measure

How many people sign up in the first day, how many invite a teammate in the first week, and how many open a shared doc together.

## Open questions

Do we show the price in the post, or only on the page? Do we launch the phone app the same day?
