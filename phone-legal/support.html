Getting started

Agentbox for iPhone signs in to the account you made in Agentbox on your computer. If you do not have one yet, download Agentbox at agentbox.ac/download, sign up there, then sign in on your iPhone with the same email and password.

If you signed up on your computer with Google, choose Forgot password? on the iPhone's sign-in page to set a password for the same account.

Reporting a problem from the app

In the app, open Settings, then General, then Send feedback. It reaches the people who make Agentbox.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Settings, then General, then Delete account. You can also ask us at {{CONTACT}}.
