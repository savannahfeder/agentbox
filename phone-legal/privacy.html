Privacy Policy

Agentbox for iPhone and the Agentbox account. Last updated 24 October 2026.

This policy says what Agentbox for iPhone and the Agentbox account it signs in to keep about you, where it is kept, who else receives it, and how to delete it. Agentbox runs coding agents on your own computer; the iPhone app lets you read and answer them while you are away from it.

1. Who we are

Agentbox is provided by {{OPERATOR}} ("we", "us"). You can reach us at {{CONTACT}}.

2. Your account

To use the app you make an Agentbox account. It holds your email address, the name you give, and, if you sign in with Google or Apple, the name and picture that service shares with us. Your password is handled by our hosting provider's sign-in service (section 5) and we never see it in plain text. If you sign in with Google or Apple, that service tells us who you are; it does not give us your password.

3. What your computer puts online for your phone

The Agentbox app on your computer writes the following to your account so your phone can read it while the computer is closed. You choose which projects go, in the app's Settings, on the Phone page.

  • Copies of threads. For each project on your phone: each thread's title, state and the latest part of its conversation, what an agent is doing while it runs, and what an agent asks your permission to do. Long text is cut. Keys, passwords and other values that look like secrets are replaced with "[hidden]" before they leave your computer, and the path of your home folder is written as "~".
  • The files those threads name. Pictures, films, sounds, pages and documents that a thread from the last 7 days names, from that project's own folders only, up to 25 MB a file and 10 GB for each computer. Files named for secrets, and keys, certificates and databases, never go. Words in text files have their secrets hidden as above.
  • A copy of your computer's settings, so the phone's Settings pages can show them: switches and choices, your project names, and the email addresses of the Claude Code and Codex accounts signed in on that computer. It carries no folder paths and none of your instructions.
  • The project names of the projects you put on your phone, and when your computer last wrote them.

4. What your phone sends

What you do in the app is written to your account for your computer to carry out: replies, new threads and the files you attach to them, answers to permission requests, closing or putting off a thread, and changes to your computer's settings. Your computer reads them the next time it is on and does them as if you had done them at the computer.

Dictation. When you press the microphone, the app listens until you press Stop or Send and writes what you say into your message. Your iPhone turns your voice into text on your iPhone whenever it can. When it cannot, your voice goes to Apple's speech recognition service, as it does when you dictate with the iPhone's own keyboard, and Apple's privacy policy applies to it. The app keeps no recording, and neither we nor Supabase receive your voice; only the words you send reach your account, like anything you type.

Your words do not go to an AI company from the app. Your computer hands them to the coding agent you choose, Claude Code (made by Anthropic) or Codex (made by OpenAI), signed in to your own account with that company, and that company's own terms and privacy policy apply to what it receives. If you have an Agentbox Cloud plan and your computer is off, Agentbox Cloud does this in its place, with the same agent on the same account (section 5).

5. Where it is kept, and who else receives it

  • Supabase hosts your account, the copies above and the files, in the United States (US East, North Virginia). Each row and each file is readable and changeable by your own account only, enforced by the database's own rules, except what you share with a team or a room (section 6).
  • Google or Apple, if you sign in with them.
  • Apple, your voice while you dictate, only when your iPhone cannot turn it into text by itself (section 4).
  • Amazon Web Services, if you have an Agentbox Cloud plan. When your computer is off, a reply you send runs on a cloud machine we run in the United States (US East, North Virginia, us-east-1). It receives a copy of the thread's folder, its conversation and your reply, and what the agent makes there goes back to your computer the next time it is on. These copies are deleted with the run's storage within a week.
  • Expo and Apple, if you turn on alerts: the app asks Expo for a push token for your phone, and alerts reach your phone through Expo's push service and Apple's Push Notification service. An alert carries the title of the thread that needs you and, for a permission request, what the agent asks to run.

We do not sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites. The app contains no advertising or analytics software.

6. Teams

If you join a team, your teammates see your name, email address and picture, the projects and messages you share with the team, the invitations you send, and a short summary of each of your threads (its state, its project and the summary the thread already has). A thread you mark private shows that you are working on something, without any of its words. If you turn on Rooms, the people in a room you join see the parts of your profile you choose to show them there.

7. How long we keep it

We keep what is in your account for as long as the account exists, with these exceptions: a thread's copy is removed when it is no longer carried to your phone; a file is removed three days after no thread names it any more, and the oldest threads' files are removed first when a computer's space is full.

8. Deleting your account

In the app, open Settings, then General, then Delete account. This removes your files online, your account, the copies and settings your computers wrote, what you sent from your phone, and everything you wrote in shared projects. A project or team your teammates still use is handed to one of them; one nobody else uses is deleted. It cannot be undone. Your threads stay on your computer. You can also ask us to delete your account at {{CONTACT}}.

9. Your rights

You can see and change most of what we keep in the app itself, and you can ask us at {{CONTACT}} for a copy of your information, to correct it or to delete it. If you are in the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area, you also have the right to object to or restrict how we use it and to complain to your data protection authority. We use your information to provide the service you ask for, which is our legal basis for it.

10. Children

Agentbox is not meant for children under 13, or under 16 in the European Economic Area, and we do not knowingly keep their information. If you believe a child has made an account, tell us at {{CONTACT}} and we will delete it.

11. Security

Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your files are kept in private storage and reached through links that expire. No method of keeping information is perfect, and we cannot promise it will never be reached by someone who should not.
