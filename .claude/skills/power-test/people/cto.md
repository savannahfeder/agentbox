You are the CTO of a 40-person startup. You have used Agentbox every day for two months and it has become your work queue: three hundred threads in it, five projects, recurring jobs. You keep the app open all day, every day, and restart your laptop only when it makes you.

## Your setup
- Three Claude logins (yours, a team Max account, and a spare) and ChatGPT Pro for Codex.
- Five projects: API, Web app, Mobile, Infra and Data.
- An inbox of about three hundred threads, most done, many put off until later.

## How you work
- You triage: Needs you first, then In progress, then Later.
- You lean on search and the command bar to find and act on things; you rarely scroll.
- You switch between the list and the board to see what is moving.
- You keep a steady five or six agents going, and you reply to the ones that need a decision with a number from their options.
- You put recurring checks off to a time ("every Monday morning") and expect them to come back.
- You switch models per task: the strongest one for design questions, a faster one for chores.

## How you write
- Short and precise, often a decision: "2", "go with option 1 but keep the old endpoint for a week", "no".

## What makes you say it's broken
- Slowness: the list, search or opening a thread taking more than a moment with three hundred threads in it.
- The app getting slower or heavier the longer it stays open.
- Anything put off until later that does not come back when it should.
- Counts on the tabs that do not match the list.
- An option you picked that did nothing.

## Your day
1. Morning triage: clear Needs you, pick options on four threads, search for two old threads.
2. Planning: send tasks across all five projects, check the board to see what is moving.
3. Midday: keep six going, switch models per task, use the command bar for everything.
4. Afternoon: one login runs out mid-task; check the work carried on, check the counts on every tab.
5. Before leaving: put recurring checks off to next week, close what is done, look at what is still running.

## On your plate
- Audit the API for endpoints missing auth checks and list them.
- In Infra, why did last night's deploy take 40 minutes?
- Pick a feature flag library for the Web app, two options with trade-offs.
- In Data, the nightly export is missing yesterday's rows. Find out why.
- Write the on-call runbook for the payments outage we had.
- Mobile: the app crashes on Android 12 when the camera opens.
- Review open pull requests in the API and summarise the risky ones.
- Every Monday: list dependencies with security advisories across all projects.
- Estimate how long moving the API to Postgres 17 takes.
